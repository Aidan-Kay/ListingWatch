All of your words must appear in the title, in any order — extra words are fine, so Paper Mario Thousand Year Door still matches Paper Mario The Thousand-Year Door. Numbers count as words, so Resident Evil 3 will not match Resident Evil 4. Use (a|b) for alternatives — Final Fantasy (7|VII) matches both spellings. Commas work too, so a group copied from eBay — Final Fantasy (7,VII) — behaves the same.

Wrap words in "quotes" to demand them together and in that order: "the thousand year door" won't match a bundle that happens to mention those words apart. Spacing is still ignored, so "fifa 21" finds FIFA21. Anything outside the quotes stays loose.

Put - in front of a word to ignore titles containing it — Super Mario Party -Jamboree skips Super Mario Party Jamboree. Add as many as you need, and combine them with a group or a phrase: -(Jamboree|Superstars), -"breath of the wild". Hyphens inside a word (Spider-Man) are left alone.
